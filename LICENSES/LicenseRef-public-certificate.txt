Public certificates, redistributed as test fixtures

The files this identifier covers are X.509 certificates that their issuers
publish for anyone to fetch, store and relay, because relaying them is what a
certificate is for: CA certificates published by the Internet Security
Research Group (Let's Encrypt) at https://letsencrypt.org/certificates/, and
an end-entity certificate served to every TLS client and logged in public
Certificate Transparency logs.

pkinative claims no copyright in them and grants no licence to them. They are
committed unmodified, byte for byte as published, so that a parser bug cannot
be baked into both a fixture and its reading. Where each came from, when, its
SHA-256 and the terms under which it is published are recorded in
tests/fixtures/PROVENANCE.md, which tests/docs/fixture-budget.test.ts holds to
the files.
